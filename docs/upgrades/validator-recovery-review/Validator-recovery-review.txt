WHAT THE UPGRADE DOES
The old CryptoDungeon operator key remains frozen. At the approved activation,
the handler removes that operator's existing authorizations, redirects its
withdrawal destination, and grants 13 limited SDK message types to the selected
recovery controller ending 0653lh. The validator identity, current consensus
key, staking status, funds and third-party delegations are preserved. There is
no automatic fund transfer or commission withdrawal. Other validators are outside
this recovery's scope.
